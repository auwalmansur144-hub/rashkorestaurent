Our Story

A House of Hospitality

"Ahlan wa sahlan — you are family, and the table is yours."

Rashko opened its doors in kano with a single ambition: to bring the unhurried rhythm of an Arabian kitchen to Kano. Our chefs trained and well respected, and they cook the way the best dishes in kano with patience, generosity, and respect for the season.

The Meat and Chickens are locally sourced and slow-roasted for best results. The spices are blended in-house, ground fresh each morning.

We have private areas for you to eat confortablely, and fast services for Delivery and Takeaway.
